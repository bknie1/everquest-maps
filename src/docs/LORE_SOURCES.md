# Lore sources

Reference index for the [History of Norrath](http://lorenorrath.free.fr/index.php) archive
compiled by Lumena Naktame of Traders of The Edge: EverQuest and EverQuest II in-game books
gathered and annotated in one place.

Cite it, don't copy from it. The site states that all logos, images and texts are
copyrighted by their respective owners.

Pages are reached as `http://lorenorrath.free.fr/read.php?link=<slug>`.

## Gods

One page per deity, at `gods_<name>`: `gods_innoruuk`, `gods_cazicthule`,
`gods_solusekro`, `gods_ralloszek`, `gods_tunare`, `gods_veeshan`, `gods_brellserilis`,
`gods_bristlebane`, `gods_bertoxxulous`, and the rest. Relevant wherever a zone carries a
temple or shrine that wants its sigil drawn correctly, as Innoruuk's did for Neriak.

## Also on the site

The archive has its own [maps](http://lorenorrath.free.fr/maps.php) and
[sources](http://lorenorrath.free.fr/sources.php) pages, and an EverQuest II timeline. The
maps there are EQ2-era and not a substitute for the EQ1 geometry this atlas is built on,
but the sources page is worth following for anything that needs a primary citation.
