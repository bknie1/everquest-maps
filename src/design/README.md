# Design & archive

Review material and reference, kept in the repo so the atlas can be picked apart
and rebuilt without hunting for anything.

| folder | contents |
|---|---|
| `kits/` | catalogue sheets for every asset module — what each piece looks like, at a glance |
| `zones/` | a render of all 79 zones, every layer composited, for review and note-taking |
| `alignment/` | the EQOA / EQ1 layer: positions, coverage report, conventions |
| `sources/` | upstream map packs the rebuilds are checked against |

## `kits/`

- `fauna_kit.png` — 15 races and 5 creatures. Ears are the through-line: dark elf
  longest, high and wood elf shorter, halflings smallest but still pointed, gnomes
  round, kerrans on top of the head.
- `flora_kit.png` — 6 trees and 6 kinds of undergrowth.
- `darkelf_kit.png` — the Teir'Dal set: gates, temple, library, Lodge of the Dead,
  bastion, rune wall, graffiti, crest, triquetra.
- `terrain_kit.png` — peak, rock band, grass field.

## `zones/`

One PNG per zone with base, markers, decoration and places composited, so a sheet
shows what a player actually sees. Rendered on the same off-white as the game
client. These are for review: mark them up and send them back.

## What the renders will not tell you

Preview renders read heavier than the game does — line weight is uniform here,
where the client thins it. When density looks marginal on a sheet it is usually
fine in game, so verify before thinning anything.
