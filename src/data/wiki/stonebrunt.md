Stonebrunt Mountains
From EverQuest Legends Wiki
The Stonebrunt Mountains is a very spiritual place. It is the home of the Kejekan people, who rule over the land atop Mount Klaw, within the small fortress of Kejek. Recently, Heretics from Paineel and Kobolds from the Warrens have found ways into the area and have started causing trouble for the kejekans.

Dangers
This is a vast zone, with many mountains and ravines, and it is very easy to stumble across an aggressive creature while travelling across it or hunting within it. It is important to stay aware of your surroundings at all times.
Giant creatures known as the Ancients wander through the area. They are very powerful, and are immune to things like root and snare. They move extremely quickly and are difficult to hunt in this area without additional monsters joining the fray.
Benefits
Stonebrunt Mountains is larger than almost all the other zones in the game. This makes it a good place to hunt without overcrowding or fighting for monsters. There are many camps (mostly of kobolds) and also several open areas where you can hunt freely. The only downside is that there are also vast areas of nothing, where you could stand for a day and only see a single monster walk by.
In the town of Kejek, you can find some tradeskill equipment as well as merchants and a place to rest. Unfortunately, melee classes can't bind here, and the kejekans will not assist you against roaming monsters.
Traveling To and From
The only way to get to Stonebrunt Mountains is by travelling through Paineel and through the kobold-filled Warrens to the area where they've broken through into the mountain range. There is a dock on the shore, but it currently leads nowhere. In live Everquest, this dock is used to travel to the Gulf of Gunthak - but that is not part of Everquest Legends at this time.
The zone itself is divided into four regions:
Jungle Region - this is the southern most region, where the entrance to The Warrens and the docks are located. It is distinguishable by the jungle vegetation and trees.
Valley Region - this is the next region north. It is distinguishable by the dark green grass and snow topped trees.
Mountain Region - this is the next region north. It is distinguishable by the barren, rocky terrain.
Highlands Region - this is the northern most region. It is distinguishable by the snowy ground.
