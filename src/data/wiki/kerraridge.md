Were you looking for the zone Kerra Island?
Overview
The inhabitants of Kerra Island. These cat like people are indifferent to most everyone with the notable exception of Erudites and some other evil races. The Erudites tend to treat the Kerrans as nothing more than animals, and have stolen a number of Kerran artifacts. It is not known at this time which of these races arrived first on their shared continent.
