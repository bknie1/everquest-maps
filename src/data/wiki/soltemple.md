The Temple of Solusek Ro
From EverQuest Legends Wiki
(Redirected from Temple of Solusek Ro)
The Temple of Solusek Ro ("Sol Ro") is a unique zone, new to the world of Norrath. It is a zone not for adventuring and hunting, killing monsters or selling goods, but for interacting with the inhabitants of the Temple. These inhabitants hail from all of the different classes and races, and are present here for one practical purpose, to give out quests. The Temple was added in the October 1999 patch and was opened along with Plane of Hate and removal of classic item lists.

Traveling To and From
The Temple of Solusek Ro can be found by traveling through the zone of the Lavastorm Mountains. When you travel to the north middle of that zone, along the northern edge of the lava pit known as Solusek's Eye, there is a small walkway and a tunnel that leads into the Temple.
