Launch the Map Previewer →

Runs entirely in your browser — no install, nothing uploaded. You pick the folder; it never leaves your machine.

How the previewer works

  1. Download the map pack from the latest release and extract it.
  2. Click Launch the Map Previewer above.
  3. Hit Open maps folder… and point it at your Emoda Legends Maps folder.
  4. Browse: filter zones, toggle layers (base / POI / deco / historical), labels on or off, drag to pan, wheel to zoom.
