## New doctrine from this review

- **Titles are too big in some cases. The bar is unrest's SMALLER title size.**
- **Standardize compass size** (everfrost's is called out as a good "standard").
- **Compasses live in the margin**, padded and knocked out, never interfering
  with borders (splitpaw note, generalized).
- **No icons/figures in titles** (runnyeye goblins, 2026-09-06).
- Kaladim's letterform family is **chiseled** (not "rocky").
